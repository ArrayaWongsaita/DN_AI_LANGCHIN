Student Handbook (extract) — Sample School
Attendance: Students must attend at least 80 percent of classes to be eligible for final examinations.
Late policy: Students arriving after 08.30 are marked late. Three late marks count as one absence.
Uniform: Students wear the school uniform Monday to Thursday and the scout uniform on Friday.
Contact: Questions about the curriculum can be sent to the academic office during working hours, Monday to Friday, 08.00 to 16.00.
